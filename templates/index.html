Understanding Centrality

Degree Centrality

Measures the number of direct connections a node has.

Betweenness Centrality

Measures how often a node lies on shortest paths between other nodes.

Closeness Centrality

Measures how close a node is to other reachable nodes.

Eigenvector Centrality

Gives importance to nodes connected to other important nodes.

Centrality values should be interpreted in the context of the network and the selected measure.
